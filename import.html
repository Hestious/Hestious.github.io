Prepare your ZIP

  • Download your project’s source ZIP from Overleaf, or zip the project folder.
  • Include the .tex files, bibliography, and figures used by the document.
  • A file named main.tex is preferred. A single document containing \documentclass can also be detected.
  • Keep included files and figures at their original relative paths.

What to expect

The web version supports ordinary text, headings, lists, and mathematical formulas. It is a reading version, so its layout may differ from your compiled PDF.

Special packages, complex custom commands, and diagrams such as TikZ may need adjustment. Keep a PDF alongside the web note when exact typesetting matters.

If conversion fails, the previous published website stays available. Open the build status for the error details.
